Add `loom` model tests for concurrent publish, unpublish, reuse and read, a unit test showing that publishing a dead token does nothing, a `loom` model of a snapshot racing a free and reuse of its block, and unit tests for capacity exhaustion. Add the `loom` command to the `package` job beside `cargo test`. Verify that `cargo test --manifest-path native/shared-index/Cargo.toml` and `RUSTFLAGS="--cfg loom" cargo test --release --manifest-path native/shared-index/Cargo.toml` succeed.
- [ ] 3.2 Extend `SharedCacheOwner` in `benchmarks/stream_cost/shared_cache/coordinator.py` with the following: creating the index; the arena payload store; the control-role `hello` from [Control connection](design.md#control-connection), sending the read-only index descriptor and the session's ring descriptor with the reply's first `sendmsg` and closing the owner's ring descriptor afterwards; an observer that publishes at the admission commit point, unpublishes, and rewrites epoch and availability words; progress publication from `UpstreamProgress`; and ring draining in `_sweep`. Extend `tests/shared_cache/test_owner.py` with parametrized cases for publication after a committed admission, no publication after a rolled-back admission, unpublication after a write, a reset and an eviction, availability rewrite, descriptor passing over a partial send, ring overflow, and an RPC `select-identity` hit whose entry is evicted and whose block is reused before the reply is encoded. That case is injected deterministically by wrapping the owner core's `WeightedLru.touch`, and the reply must be a miss or the original bytes, never other bytes. Verify with `just pytest -- tests/shared_cache/test_owner.py`.
- [ ] 3.3 Extend `attachment.py` and `adapters.py` with a `shared-index` mode that opens the [control connection](design.md#control-connection) in both execution models. Implement the handshake, descriptor ownership and transition exactly as designed: a blocking `socket.recv_fds` loop with `MSG_CMSG_CLOEXEC` (run through `asyncio.to_thread` in asyncio workers), read-only index mapping, then an EOF-only daemon thread (synchronous) or an EOF-only task on `asyncio.open_unix_connection(sock=...)` (asyncio) that sets the detached flag. RPC connections keep their current readers and handshake. `_find_one_by_id` tries the local hit first and falls back to `select-identity` when the entry is absent. Extend `tests/shared_cache/test_adapter_paths.py`, parametrized over synchronous and asyncio adapters, for local hits, fallback hits, misses with admission, metadata-epoch mismatch, detachment, RPC replies staying intact while the watcher runs, a handshake reply split across reads, and rejected handshakes (missing or extra descriptors) that leave no received descriptor open. Verify with `just pytest -- tests/shared_cache/test_adapter_paths.py`.
- [ ] 3.4 Add `tests/shared_cache/test_shared_index_oracle.py`, a Hypothesis stateful test of the differential requirement in [the delta spec](specs/shared-cache-benchmarking/spec.md). It runs a real `CacheCore` with a small budget, so that evictions occur, plus the owner observer and a worker-side reader in the same process. Rules cover admission, identity write, namespace reset, availability toggle and progress expiry. After every step, an invariant compares each known key's local result with `lookup_identity_encoded`: a local value must equal the core's value, or the local result is absent. Faker generates incidental documents. Verify with `just pytest -- tests/shared_cache/test_shared_index_oracle.py -n 0`.
- [ ] 3.5 Add the local-validation cases to `tests/shared_cache/test_scenarios.py`, reusing its owner and worker fixtures and parametrizing where cases share logic:
  - owner SIGKILL, with local hits stopping within 100 ms;
  - owner SIGSTOP and a stalled watch with the owner alive, with hits stopping by the 3-second expiry;
  - attachment to a replacement owner, with no reads of the old mapping;
  - a worker killed mid-read while the owner reuses the space;
  - a multi-process torn-read stress, in which every served payload must equal one published for its key;
  - publication-capacity exhaustion, with bounded mapping size and RPC fallback;
  - a write that the owner processes between an admission's validity check and its commit, injected deterministically by wrapping the owner core's `WeightedLru.conditional_put`, after which no worker serves the entry locally;
  - an eviction between `conditional_put` and the commit, injected the same way, after which the entry is never published;
  - attempts by a worker to write through the received index descriptor, map it writable, upgrade its read-only mapping with `mprotect`, or write through a descriptor reopened read-write via `/proc/self/fd`, each of which must fail.

  Verify with `just pytest -- tests/shared_cache`.
