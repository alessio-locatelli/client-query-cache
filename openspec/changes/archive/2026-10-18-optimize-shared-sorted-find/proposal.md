# Proposal

## Why

[Issue #290](https://github.com/alessio-locatelli/client-query-cache/issues/290) identifies sorted-find owner coordination as a remaining cost in the v15 study. Its acceptance permits an optimization or an evidence-backed decision against expanding support.

## What Changes

- Evaluate local publication of completed bounded research cursor results.
- Record the benefit and limits against the existing direct and independent baselines.
- Keep public cursor integration under [#295](https://github.com/alessio-locatelli/client-query-cache/issues/295).

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `shared-cache-benchmarking`: bounded cursor publication and write invalidation in research adapters.

## Impact

Internal shared adapters, residency publication, cursor correctness coverage and a reproducible research report. The public API and native layout remain unchanged.
